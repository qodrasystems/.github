# Code of Conduct — Qodra Systems

**Qodra Systems** is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and high-craft engineering community across all repositories and discussions.

## Our Standards

- Communicate constructively, respectfully, and concisely.
- Focus feedback on code, architecture, and user outcomes—never on individuals.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Report unacceptable behavior or concerns to [info@qodrasystems.com](mailto:info@qodrasystems.com).
